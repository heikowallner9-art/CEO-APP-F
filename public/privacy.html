Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 13, 2026

AI CEO Executive Command Center ("the App") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we handle information when you use our application.

1. Information Collection

The App is designed as a local-first application. Most data, including your executive directives, goals, and habit tracking, is stored locally on your device. We do not collect personal identifiers like your name or email unless you explicitly provide them through a support request.

2. AI and Data Processing

The App uses Artificial Intelligence to provide executive insights. While your inputs are processed to generate responses, we do not use your personal executive data to train global AI models. Data transmitted for AI processing is handled via secure, encrypted channels.

3. Third-Party Services

The App is hosted on Vercel. Vercel may collect standard web analytics (such as IP addresses and browser types) to ensure service stability and performance. Please refer to Vercel's Privacy Policy for more details.

4. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data. However, as a local-first app, the security of your data also depends on the security of your individual device.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.

6. Reporting Inappropriate Content

In compliance with Microsoft Store Policy 11.16, we provide a mechanism for users to report inappropriate AI-generated content. You can report such content by clicking the "Report Inappropriate AI Content" link within the App or by emailing us directly at heikowallner9@gmail.com.

7. Contact Us
